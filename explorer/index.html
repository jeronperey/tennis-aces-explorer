Which players win by serving big, and which win without it?

One dot per ATP player, 2015–2024. Pick a starting view, change what the x-axis shows, filter by court, drag a box around a group, or click a dot for that player.

About the data and the measures

Measures. Ace rate is aces as a share of a player's service points. Serve points won and return points won are the shares of points won on their own serve and on the opponent's serve. Win rate is the share of matches won. The purple dashed line is a straight-line trend, and r shows how closely the two measures move together (1 = perfectly, 0 = not at all).

Who is shown. Players with at least 50 matches (30 on a given court), 2015–2024, men's tour-level matches only; use the minimum-matches slider to raise the bar. Unfinished matches and matches on other surfaces are left out.
