Which players win by serving big, and which win without it?

One dot per ATP player (2015–2024, at least 50 matches). Change what the x-axis shows, filter by court, drag a box around a group, or click a dot for that player's profile.

How to read it. Ace rate is aces as a share of a player's service points. Serve points won and return points won are shares of points won on their own serve and on the opponent's serve. Win rate is the share of matches won. The dashed purple line is a straight-line trend and r shows how strongly the two measures move together (1 = perfectly, 0 = not at all).

Careful. Only players with 50+ matches are shown, so this is a survivor sample. Courts show players with 30+ matches there. This shows association, not cause. Men's ATP tour-level matches only.
